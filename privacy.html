MORTAL

Privacy Policy

MORTAL is made by CEKMEDIA LLC, California. Last updated: November 11, 2026

MORTAL is a multiplayer game for Meta Quest 3 made by CEKMEDIA LLC ("we"). This policy says what the game collects, why, how long we keep it, and how to get it deleted. It covers the game and its game servers. This website collects nothing: no cookies, no analytics.

What we collect

  • A device identifier. The game server uses an identifier of your headset to recognise you between sessions, so your capacity is still yours when you come back (your body is not: you come back without one). The server keeps it in its save file and its log. It is not your Meta account; we do not receive your Meta account name, email or friends.
  • Your in-game name. Shown to other players above the body you are in. There is no way to choose one yet: you show as "mind-" and a number.
  • Time near other players. The server counts the seconds your body spends within 6 m of each other player's in the same world, and keeps the totals, to show who would notice when you leave a body.
  • Your game state. The world, bodies, capacities, ages and positions the server needs to run the game.
  • Voice. The game asks for the microphone the first time you are in a body. When you allow it, your microphone audio is sent live through the game server to the players in the same world as you, and they hear it fade with distance. Silence is not sent. It is not recorded or stored; it exists only while it is being relayed. You can mute any player at any time. To stop sending your voice, deny the microphone permission (Quest Settings > Apps > Vacate > Permissions); you still hear others.
  • Reports. If you report a player, the server stores the time, your name, their name and the world, so we can act on it. Blocks are kept by the server so a blocked player can't take a body you left in the last 10 minutes.
  • Network data. The server sees your IP address while you are connected, to send you game data. We do not log it.

We do not collect your real name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera or passthrough images, room scans, payment details, or advertising identifiers. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the game.

Why

Only to run the game: recognise your mind, show your name, show who would notice a body you left, relay your voice to nearby players, keep the world saved, and handle reports and blocks. We do not sell or rent data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

  • Other players see your in-game name and the body you are in, and hear your voice when in the same world.
  • Unity Relay (Unity Technologies), when you host or join with a six-letter code, carries encrypted game traffic between headsets: Unity privacy policy.
  • Meta processes data under its own policy for the Quest platform and store: Meta privacy policy.
  • Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Game state and your device identifier: while the world exists, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Reports: up to 90 days after the report is resolved, then deleted.
  • Voice: not kept at all.

Deleting your data

Email contact@cekmedia.net with the name you use in game. We delete your device identifier, name, time-near totals and game state within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting the game from your headset removes everything stored on the headset.

Children

Meta does not allow anyone under 10 on Quest. MORTAL is for ages 13 and up because players can talk to strangers. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 is playing, email us and we will delete their data.

Security

Game traffic through Unity Relay is encrypted (DTLS). Local-network play is not. If we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell affected players and the authorities the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and post the change here.
