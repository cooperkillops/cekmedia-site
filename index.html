MORTAL

You are one mind. Bodies are scarce. Leaving one is a real risk.

A multiplayer game for Meta Quest 3 and 3S. In development.

You wake inside a stranger. Everyone around you is a person, and so is every body worth wanting. Pinch another one and you slide into it. The body you left stays behind, empty, and for a while you cannot go back.

Every body carries a number, its capacity. Move into a lower one and you drop to it, for good. The terminals that stand in each world list every body in every world. Nothing floats in front of you but one line of text.

Bodies age. When yours starts dying you get a minute to find another, or you are thrown out with half of what you were. Other minds want the same empty bodies you do, and two of you reaching for one at the same moment means one of you is left stranded.

Four worlds: a New York block at night, a redwood grove, and a hab on Mars and a harbour at dusk that stay sealed until players give enough of themselves to open them. Your voice comes from the body you are in, and only your world hears it.
