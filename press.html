Description

MORTAL is a multiplayer game for Meta Quest 3 about owning nothing but where you are. You wake inside a stranger's body. Pinch another body and you move into it; the one you left stays behind, empty and locked, and anyone else can take it before you get back. Every body has a capacity. Move into a lower one and you drop to it for good. Bodies age and die, new ones are born at the nursery, and every moving body in the world is another player. There are no bots. Four worlds, two of them sealed until players pay into them; your voice carries only as far as the body you are in.

Why it's interesting

  • Loss that stays lost. No undo, no saved favourite body. The world keeps what you leave behind.
  • Every body is a person. No NPCs pretend to be players. An empty body is an empty body; a moving one is someone.
  • Information lives in places. Lists of bodies are on terminals standing in the world; the only screen text is one line.
  • Your voice goes with you. Proximity voice from the body you're in, heard only in your world; mute and block in the hand menu.
